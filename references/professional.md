# Professional mode / 邮件与职场沟通

Use for emails, DMs, application materials, interview follow-ups, and work messages.

- State the reason for writing early and make the request easy to identify.
- Be polite without repeated apologies, excessive hedging, or ceremonial openings.
- Match relationship and context: colleague, supervisor, editor, unfamiliar researcher, recruiter.
- Keep necessary gratitude and tact; don't strip warmth just to be short.
- Avoid “I hope this email finds you well” by default, not as an absolute prohibition.
- Avoid inventing familiarity, prior contact, commitments, or urgency.
- Preserve concrete details, dates, names, and asks.
- For sensitive follow-ups, give the recipient a graceful way to have missed the message.

Quick check: Is this something the sender could plausibly send without sounding scripted?
